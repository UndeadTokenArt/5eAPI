Draw on the Debilitating Power of the Void

The Shadow Domain deals with the powers of shadow and the Void—a sinister mirror of the life-giving powers of the Light. Clerics who embrace these powers can wither their enemies’ flesh and debilitate their minds.

Level 3: Shadow Spells
Your connection to this domain ensures you always have certain spells ready. When you reach a Cleric level specified in the Shadow Domain Spells table, you thereafter always have the listed spells prepared.

Level 3: Shadowform
As a Bonus Action, you can expend a use of your Channel Divinity to take on a shadowy form that grants you the following benefits:

Shadow Stealth. While you are in an area of Dim Light or Darkness, you can take the Hide action as a Bonus Action.
Void Empowerment. When you cast a spell using a spell slot that deals Psychic or Necrotic damage, you can add 1d6 to one damage roll of the spell.
The form lasts for 1 minute, but it ends early if you dismiss it (no action required), if you have the Incapacitated condition, or if you deal Radiant damage with a spell.

Level 3: Devouring Plague
When a creature you can see within 30 feet of you fails a saving throw, you can cause that creature to take 1d10 Necrotic damage. If you do, you regain Hit Points equal to the Necrotic damage dealt.

You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum of once), and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.

Level 6: Dispersion
When you are hit by an attack roll, you can take a Reaction to disperse into shadowy energy. Until the end of your next turn, you have Resistance to all damage (including the damage from the triggering attack); you can’t take any action, Bonus Action, or Reaction; and you have a Fly Speed of 40 feet. When the effect ends, you can spend up to half of your total Hit Point Dice, regaining Hit Points as if you had finished a Short Rest.

Once you use this feature, you can’t use it again until you finish a Short or Long Rest.

Level 17: Vampiric Embrace
While your Shadowform is active, whenever you roll 1d6 to add extra damage to a spell, one creature of your choice within 30 feet of you regains that many Hit Points.
